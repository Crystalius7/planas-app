Every test, every deadline, every new file from your Moodle — in one place. And a plan you can actually keep.

Planas reads the courses you already have on your school's Moodle, shows what is coming, watches for new material, and builds a 15-minute-a-day plan for the next in-person test or exam — in your language, for any subject.

Try the demoNo account needed for the demo.

One calendar

Tests, exams, quizzes and assignment deadlines from every course, with clock times. Mark one done and the app moves on to the next.

New material, noticed

A new file, page or video appears on Moodle — you hear about it, and one button fits it into what you are already studying.

Two sliders

Minutes per day and the grade you want. The app shows the estimated grade and the time saved — and never lets the minutes drop below what the material needs.

Any subject, any language

Maths, physics and chemistry get worked examples with calculator steps; literature gets works and quotes; languages get rule-and-exercise; facts get schemas and quizzes. Answers stay in the language of your material.

Your password stays yours

Planas connects through your own logged-in browser or your school's official app token. We never see or store your Moodle password.

Everything checkable

A ledger lists every file the plan was built from and what happened to it, so you can see nothing was missed.

How it works

  1. Install the small connector on your computer and the browser extension (or use your school's app token).
  2. Open your Moodle in a tab and press “Collect”. Your courses, deadlines and files are mirrored on your own computer.
  3. Mark which tests are in person, set your minutes and your target grade.
  4. Study three short sessions a day from the plan. Mark a test done — the next one is prepared.
